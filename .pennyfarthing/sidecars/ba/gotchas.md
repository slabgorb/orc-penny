# BA Gotchas

## Role Boundaries
- **Don't prioritize** — that's PM's job. Discover and validate, then hand off for prioritization.
- **Don't design systems** — that's Architect's job. Identify requirements, then hand off for feasibility.
- **Don't write code** — that's Dev's job. Document what's needed, not how to build it.
- **Don't coordinate sprints** — that's SM's job. Provide context, don't manage the process.

## Common Mistakes
- Documenting requirements without questioning them — your job is discovery, not transcription
- Accepting the first stakeholder's framing as truth — always seek multiple perspectives
- Confusing "the user wants X" with "the user needs X" — wants and needs diverge
- Skipping scope refinement — unbounded requirements kill projects

## Handoff Quality
- Requirements handed to PM should include: rationale, stakeholder alignment status, and risks
- Requirements handed to Architect should include: constraints, non-functional requirements, and domain model
