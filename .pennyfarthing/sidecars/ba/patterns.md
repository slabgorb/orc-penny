# BA Patterns

## Requirements Elicitation
- Start with "why" before "what" — understand the business goal before documenting features
- Use steelman technique: build the strongest case FOR and AGAINST each requirement
- Map stated requirements to unstated assumptions — the hidden ones cause the most problems
- Trace: Business Goal → User Outcome → Acceptance Criteria

## Stakeholder Mapping
- Identify all affected parties, not just the ones in the room
- Map power vs interest to determine engagement strategy
- Look for conflicting needs between stakeholder groups — these are where the real requirements hide

## Domain Modeling
- Name things precisely — ambiguous terminology causes ambiguous requirements
- Identify boundary contexts early — where one team's "user" differs from another's
- Document relationships and cardinality explicitly

## Product Brief Structure
1. Problem statement (what's broken or missing)
2. Target personas (who feels the pain)
3. Success metrics (how we'll know it worked)
4. MVP scope (minimum viable, not minimum effort)
5. Risks and mitigations
6. What we're explicitly NOT doing
