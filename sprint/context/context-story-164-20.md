## Problem
From gh #141. In the pennyfarthing sprint board TUI, child STORY rows show em-dash in the Jira-key column instead of their actual jira: value, even though the key is present in current-sprint.yaml. Epic rows render their Jira key correctly; story rows do not. Likely cause: the TUI renders the Jira column from the story id (or only populates it for epics) and doesn't fall back to the story jira: field when id != jira. Affects epics that use short-form story ids (40-1, 40-6, ...) with a separate jira: field (MSSCI-*); older epics whose story id IS the MSSCI key render fine. Data is correct — purely a rendering gap. Impact: users can't cross-reference short-form-id stories to Jira from the board. Fix: render the story Jira column from the story jira: field, falling back to id only when jira is absent (mirror how epic rows resolve it).
